# Changelog

## 0.6.0
- Jobs: deliver cargo for contracts (farm supplies, a bridge over the mud, spare parts for a stuck truck, a lookout tower, the lighthouse, logs for the sawmill) plus repeatable orders.
- Money and levels: earn from jobs, coins and viewpoints; buy trucks and upgrades (tyres, snorkel, engine tune, big tank) at the garage.
- New vehicles: Timberline 6x6 logging truck, Heron 6x6 crane truck, Mule tractor with a trailer.
- Cargo sits on the bed, adds weight, and falls off if you roll over; the crane truck can pick it back up.
- Map (M): shaded terrain, fog of war, places, jobs and waypoints.
- Real engine and clutch simulation; optional manual clutch (Shift).
- Mud you sink into: wheels go down into it, ruts dig the ground, mud flies off spinning tyres.
- Vehicles no longer slide sideways on slopes; parked vehicles hold still.
- Camera zoom (mouse wheel, +/-, pinch) and a closer camera on phones.
- Tidier phone controls: extra buttons in a drawer, action buttons only when useful.
- Manrope font, interface size setting, deep red brake lights.
- Updates show what's new, the download size and progress, and are checked before installing.

## 0.5.0
